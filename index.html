How we choose our picks

We don’t run a lab or claim timed trials. Instead, we aggregate feedback from thousands of owners on major retail sites, hobby forums, and professional reviews. Our picks are based on long-term reliability, accessory availability, and common user complaints, wobble, overheating, chuck wear, not marketing claims. We also consider warranty support and the breadth of replacement parts. A rotary tool that holds its speed under load and resists vibration over years of use beats one with flashy specs but a short lifespan.
